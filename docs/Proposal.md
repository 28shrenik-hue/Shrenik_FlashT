# FlashTile — AI-Powered Enterprise Learning Companion

Employees want to grow but long training experiences compete with the workday. FlashTile delivers personalized, bite-sized learning directly to the desktop so professional development becomes a consistent habit instead of a separate destination.

The foundation includes five-minute cards, topic selection, knowledge progress, XP, streaks, and local Excel-backed storage. The product is designed to grow into grounded AI learning paths, team challenges, recognition, and privacy-conscious insights.

FlashTile's differentiator is direct delivery: most learning platforms wait for employees to visit; **FlashTile goes to the employee**.
