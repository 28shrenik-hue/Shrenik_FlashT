# FlashTile Vision

FlashTile is an AI-ready enterprise learning companion that brings short, relevant learning moments into the flow of work. Its promise is simple: **knowledge that finds you**.

The MVP demonstrates a floating cross-platform desktop tile, five learning domains, local progress, daily XP, and streaks. Future releases add grounded AI learning paths, quizzes, team challenges, rewards, analytics, and enterprise governance.

## Guardrails

- Never request or retain customer, account, credential, secret, or confidential business data.
- Keep MVP progress local and minimal.
- Clearly identify future AI-generated content and ground it in approved sources.
- Make participation and leaderboard visibility user-controlled.
