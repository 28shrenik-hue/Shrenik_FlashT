# FlashTile Learning Method

Every FlashTile lesson follows the same evidence-informed sequence:

1. **Core Concept** — introduce one essential idea clearly and without unnecessary jargon.
2. **Practical Scenario** — show where the concept affects a realistic decision.
3. **Go Deeper** — reveal technical or operational detail for interested learners.
4. **Knowledge Check** — test understanding with a different scenario or formulation.
5. **Completion** — explain the result, award progress, and offer another flash.

## Smart Recall

After completion, the learner chooses **Got it**, **Need practice**, or
**Review later**. FlashTile stores a review date locally and later presents a
different scenario and question for the same concept. Successful recall moves the
concept through **New**, **Learning**, **Practicing**, and **Mastered**.

## Content rules

- Teach one main concept per flash.
- Explain why the concept matters before testing it.
- Use synthetic, enterprise-safe scenarios.
- Do not repeat the exact example as the quiz answer.
- Explain why the correct answer works.
- Prefer clear language, then allow optional depth.
- Keep every stage readable inside the fixed FlashTile window.
